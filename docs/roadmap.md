# Roadmap

[Play](https://livingmatter.vercel.app) · [How Jev works](jev.md) · [Docs](README.md)

## V1 · Explore with living matter

Explore a flooded observatory that responds to your intent. Real-time Jev intelligence brings living matter to life, adapting the world around you as you move.

## V2 · Plan a heist together

The matter becomes an accomplice. Enemies use conventional game logic. Gameplay design will be developed separately.

## V3 · Intelligence on both sides

Explore Jev-controlled enemies interacting with the companion, using bounded perception and fair physical rules.

V1 is available now. V2 and V3 are future directions with no announced release dates.
